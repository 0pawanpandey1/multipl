SpotSafe 360.

Interactive 360° hazard-perception training. Engineered from the ground up by Multiply for warehouse operations and logistics facilities. Workers explore a realistic goods-in bay, discover safety risks in seconds, and learn corrective actions immediately, while supervisors receive verifiable training telemetry.

Request a walkthrough Discuss a custom simulation

Built according to UK HSE warehouse-safety guidelines.

9

Real warehouse hazards in one scenario, plus one deliberate decoy to penalize blind guessing.

5

Safety categories covered: fire exits, mobile machinery, racking integrity, height, and spills.

2

Training and Time Attack modes with progressive hints and configurable timers.

0

Installs or app store downloads. Instant launch in any modern browser.

From link to lesson in four minutes.

  1. 01

    Choose mode and role

    Learn at your own pace in Training mode, or compete against team members on the live scoreboard in Time Attack.

  2. 02

    Explore the 360° bay

    A full goods-in bay in 360 degrees. Rotate the viewport, inspect aisles, scan high shelves, and identify hazards before time expires.

  3. 03

    Immediate teaching on every flag

    Clicking a hazard opens an immediate teaching briefing explaining the standard procedure and why the condition is dangerous.

  4. 04

    Telemetry and review map

    An instant summary detailing finds, misses, reaction latency, and a spatial map of where attention was concentrated.

How Multiply architected the platform.

SpotSafe 360 is proof of Multiply's philosophy: high-performance technical execution without bloated dependencies.

Browser-Native WebGL

Runs smoothly at 60fps across modern browsers with zero external plugins, heavy game engines, or mandatory app installations.

Supervisor Dashboards

Personal progress metrics for learners alongside team aggregations: completion rates, time per hazard, and most frequently missed items.

Interaction Reliability

Verified across spatial interaction sequences, state transitions, screen-reader markup, and keyboard focus traps.

Air-Gapped & Private

Capable of running completely offline from a local server. Training records remain internal without phoning home to third-party trackers.

Built for enterprise reliability.

  • Zero client rollout: share a URL or embed directly in your LMS via SCORM or xAPI.
  • Dual access model: guest play with zero registration, or authenticated accounts for audits.
  • Granular role-based controls for learners, shift supervisors, and site directors.
  • Comprehensive keyboard shortcuts for inclusive operation without mouse requirements.
  • WebXR pathway built into the core build for immersive VR headsets when needed.

Operational use cases.

  • New employee induction and orientation
  • Annual refresher training for warehouse operatives
  • Material handling and forklift operator awareness
  • Safety supervisor auditing and team coaching
  • Contractor and visitor safety briefings

Privacy notice.

This website does not store personal information or use tracking cookies, marketing analytics, or user profiling. Here is how it works.

Who we are

Multiply is an independent software engineering studio based in Kathmandu, Nepal. This website introduces our team, our client engineering services, and our flagship in-house product, SpotSafe 360. You can reach our team directly at hello@multiply.com.

What this website collects: nothing

  • No accounts, passwords, or tracking cookies exist on this marketing site.
  • No third-party analytics or behavioral trackers are loaded.
  • No advertising network cookies are set in your browser.
  • The project inquiry form operates without an intermediary database. It opens a pre-composed message directly inside your native email client, giving you complete visibility and control over what is transmitted.

The SpotSafe 360 demo

When running the SpotSafe 360 interactive simulation, your scores and session preferences reside exclusively within your own browser’s local storage on your device. No telemetry is transmitted to external servers. Clearing your browser data removes stored records entirely.

External assets

Typography is served via Google Fonts to deliver consistent typography across platforms. Google receives your IP address as part of that standard web request; no personal identifiers or cookies are shared by Multiply.

Updates

If our privacy practices change, revisions will be published on this page with an updated timestamp.
